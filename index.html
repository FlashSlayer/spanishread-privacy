Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 Last updated: October 23, 2026

Plain-English summary: Spanish Read is a language learning app. We collect your email and a username if you choose to create an account. All your progress (XP, streaks, scores) is stored only on your device and never sent anywhere. We do not sell your data, show you ads, or share your information with anyone except Supabase — the service that handles account login. You can delete your account and all its data at any time from inside the app.

1. Who We Are

Spanish Read ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application for learning Spanish through immersive daily stories. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the app.

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us at support@spanishread.com.

2. Information We Collect

A. If you create an account (optional)

Creating an account is entirely optional. If you choose to sign up, we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and allow you to log in across devices.
  • Username — a display name of your choice, shown only inside your own app profile.

This information is stored by Supabase (our authentication provider). See Section 5 for details.

B. If you use the app without an account

No personal information is collected. All data is stored locally on your device only.

C. Data stored only on your device (never transmitted)

Data What it stores Where
XP & level Your total experience points and current level Your device only
Daily streak How many consecutive days you've used the app Your device only
Quiz history Which stories and quizzes you completed today (for XP caps) Your device only
App settings Dark/light mode, sound on/off, SFX on/off, notification time, language preference Your device only
Reminder time The time you set for your daily practice reminder Your device only
Cached audio Advanced story audio downloaded for offline playback Your device's temporary storage

This device-local data is managed by Android's SharedPreferences system and is never sent to us or any third party.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

  • Account authentication — your email and password let you log in and keep your account secure.
  • Display name — your username is shown in your own Profile tab.
  • App functionality — local data (XP, streak, settings) powers the app's features.
  • Daily reminders — if you enable notifications, we schedule a local notification on your device using the time you choose. No data leaves your device for this feature.

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics tracking, or any purpose not listed above.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, access, or store any of the following:

  • Your real name, phone number, or physical address
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising IDs)
  • Usage analytics or behavior tracking
  • Microphone, camera, or contacts access
  • Payment information (the app is currently free)
  • Any data from children under the age of 13

5. Third-Party Services

Supabase (authentication & audio storage)

If you create an account, your email address and username are stored by Supabase, Inc. (supabase.com), our backend provider. Supabase stores this data on secure servers and uses it solely to authenticate your account. Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant.

Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Google Fonts

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) to display text. Google may log the font request as a standard web request. No personal data from your account is sent to Google. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

No analytics, no ads

Spanish Read contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude), and no crash reporting services. We do not track how you use the app.

6. Push Notifications

If you enable daily reminders in the Profile tab, the app schedules a local notification on your device using Android's built-in notification system. This notification is generated entirely on your device — no data is sent to external servers to deliver it.

You can disable notifications at any time by turning off the Notifications toggle in the Profile tab, or through your device's system notification settings.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Account data (email, username): Retained on Supabase's servers for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab using the "Delete Account" option. This permanently removes your email and username from Supabase's systems.

Local device data (XP, streak, settings): Stored on your device only. It is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app, or you can clear it via your device's App Settings → Storage → Clear Data.

Cached audio files: Stored in your device's temporary directory. These are cleared automatically when your device's OS manages storage, or when you clear the app's cache.

To delete your account and all associated data: Open Spanish Read → Profile tab → scroll down → tap "Delete Account." This action is immediate and cannot be undone. Alternatively, email us at support@spanishread.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update your email or username through account settings.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data at any time (see Section 7).
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt out of notifications — disable at any time in-app or via device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@spanishread.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Spanish Read is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@spanishread.com.

10. Data Security

Account authentication is handled by Supabase, which uses industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and bcrypt password hashing. Passwords are never stored in plain text and are never accessible to us.

Local device data is stored using Android's SharedPreferences, which is private to the app by default and cannot be accessed by other apps on your device.

No security system is perfect. If you discover a security issue, please report it to support@spanishread.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users through a notice in the app.

Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
